This is a self-report screening demo, not a diagnosis. Each subtype is scored out of 27 (0–9 mild, 10–18 moderate, 19–27 high), a simple scale used by this dashboard. Many people show some of these symptoms because of stress, anxiety, low mood or fatigue. A diagnosis can only be made by a qualified mental health professional.
